About Us

Meet Mr. Business.

A benevolent man with a spreadsheet and a philosophy.

The Man Behind the Business

Everyone deserves to run a business.

Mr. Business is not a title. It's a belief system. The person behind Mr. Business Financial Services built this firm on a simple premise: the tools and knowledge required to run a financially healthy business should not be locked behind jargon, expensive consultants, or a prerequisite of already being wealthy.

Too many small business owners are flying blind on their own finances — not because they're incapable, but because nobody ever explained it clearly. That's the gap Mr. Business exists to fill.

Based in Springfield, Missouri, Mr. Business Financial Services works with entrepreneurs, sole proprietors, and small-to-medium businesses who are serious about what they're building and just need a competent person in their corner who communicates like a human being.

No condescension. No runaround. Just clear numbers and honest guidance.
